baking and decorating

Baking is both an art and a science. You have to combine ingredients in just the right amount in just the right order to create the perfect something. I use this medium to visualize and build ideas in three dimensions. It’s a creative challenge to translate an abstract concept into a physical form while making every visual element precise and beautiful. I enjoy using what I make as a canvas to explore my creativity.  

An exercise in applied science and creative design
